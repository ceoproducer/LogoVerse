FAQ

How does LogoVerse work?

You describe your brand and preferences; LogoVerse builds a structured design prompt and sends it to a connected image provider. Without one, demo mode creates editable vector concepts locally.

Can I use the generated logo for my business?

Commercial rights depend on the AI provider's terms and LogoVerse's own terms, which must be published before launch. Review both and consider a trademark check. This is not legal advice.

Can I customize my logo?

Yes. The customization studio edits real SVG text and shapes live: font, colors, layout, sizes and spacing.

Can I download transparent PNG files?

Demo concepts export transparent PNGs. For AI raster output it depends on whether the provider supports transparency.

Can I download SVG files?

Demo and customized designs export genuine SVG. AI raster images are never renamed to SVG; true vectors need a vectorization workflow.

Do I need design experience?

No. Only the brand name is required.

Is LogoVerse free?

The demo is free. Plans shown are illustrative and not billed.

Who owns the generated logo?

Ownership is governed by the provider's terms and LogoVerse's terms. We make no unsupported legal promises.
